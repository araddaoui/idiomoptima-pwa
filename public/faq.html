Frequently Asked Questions

Is IdiomOptima really free?

Yes. The free tier gives you access to the full editing engine with no credit card required. Paid plans with higher limits will be available in the future.

Do you store my text?

No. Your text is sent to language processing services and is not stored on our servers. You can read our full Privacy Policy.

How does it differ from Grammarly or QuillBot?

Grammarly focuses on grammar and style rules; QuillBot paraphrases. IdiomOptima specifically targets non-native fluency. It rephrases awkward sentences to sound natural while keeping your original meaning and voice. It also respects your chosen dialect (US/UK/AU/CA) and register (academic, business, literary, general).

What file types can I use?

You can paste plain text directly into the editor. Your refined text can be exported as Word (.docx), PDF, or plain text.

Can I compare changes sentence by sentence?

Absolutely. After transformation, you can view a sentence-by-sentence comparison showing the original and refined versions side by side, with explanations for each change.

Which dialects are supported?

US, UK, Australian, and Canadian English. You can choose manually or let the app auto-detect the most appropriate dialect for your text.

Is the processing always accurate?

No language tool is perfect. While IdiomOptima uses advanced language models, we recommend you review the output. Our goal is to help, not replace your own judgment.

Do I need an account?

Not yet. The app works without any sign-up. In the future, optional accounts will be available for premium features.

How can I contact support or give feedback?

Email us at contact@IdiomOptima.com. We'd love to hear your thoughts and suggestions.
